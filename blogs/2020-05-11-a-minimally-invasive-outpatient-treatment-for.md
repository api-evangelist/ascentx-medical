Novel treatment could cure potentially dangerous condition for millions An estimated 20-30 million Europeans suffer from Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease every day. Symptoms include heartburn and regurgitation. At best uncomfortable and at worst a cause of oesophageal cancer, a novel treatment could bring relief to hundreds of millions worldwide.
